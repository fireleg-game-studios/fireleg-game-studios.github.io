Last time, we showed you the hub. This time, we take you back inside the manor.

## A hall you can read at a glance

The hall behind the front door has changed again. A coffered ceiling now frames a plaster medallion and its chandelier, the floor is chequered marble under a patterned red carpet, and portraits and candlelit console tables line the panelled walls.

{% figure "games/manor-verbier/hall-checker.webp", "The hall seen from its chequered marble floor, with a red patterned carpet, panelled walls, portraits and a coffered ceiling.", "The hall, from the floor up." %}

We place everything by walking the room in the game itself, with the same light you will carry. If a corner does not look right at lamp height, it does not stay.

## A new room: the library

The manor now has a library. Shelves of books climb the walls, a wooden ladder leans against the green wallpaper, and a tall window opens onto the night. The floor is a polished herringbone parquet that catches the lamp from far away.

{% figure "games/manor-verbier/library-window.webp", "A wooden ladder against green wallpaper beside a tall window with red curtains, in the library.", "The library, in warm amber light." %}

It is a place to slow down, look around and read what was left behind.

## Before you even step inside

The first thing you see of the manor has also changed: the front courtyard, with snow on the ground, a lantern on the wall and the great door left ajar.

{% figure "games/manor-verbier/courtyard.webp", "The manor's front courtyard at night, with snow on the ground, a lantern on the wall and the great door ajar.", "The courtyard, just before you go in." %}

## What comes next

We are still working room by room, and we would rather show you a place when it feels finished. Everything in these captures is work in progress, and none of it is final.

If you want to follow along, or tell us which room you would like to see next, our [Discord server](/discord/) is the best place to do it.
