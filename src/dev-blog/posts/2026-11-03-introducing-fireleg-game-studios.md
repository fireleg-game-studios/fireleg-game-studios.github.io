## Who we are

FIRELEG GAME STUDIOS is an independent video game studio. We build original worlds, and we don't intend to be tied to a single genre: each project should take the form that suits it best.

This blog is where we will show how our games are made: the progress, the decisions, and the things that did not work the first time.

## Our first project

MANOR VERBIER is a first-person survival horror game. You arrive at night before a manor frozen on the evening of its grand ball, the park gate closes behind you, and the only way forward is through the great door.

As things stand, you can play alone, or online with 2 to 4 players, as one of four playable characters. The horror is suggested rather than shown, and we recommend headphones.

Today, the manor is one continuous place: five levels, from the cellars to the attic, around sixty rooms, all joined by real staircases. It is built as a single map, so that the house can become a place you learn by heart.

## How we are building it

Everything in the game is either made by us or listed, with its licence, in a register we keep up to date.

We also hold ourselves to one simple habit: a change only counts once it has been verified in the game itself, with captures and measurements, and not just in an editor.
