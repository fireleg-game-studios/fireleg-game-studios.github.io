## What is MANOR VERBIER?

MANOR VERBIER is a first-person survival horror game, set in a forgotten French manor. You can explore it alone, or with up to three friends online. We are still building it, and this blog is where we show how.

## What has been happening?

Before the gate closes behind you, there is a hall. We call it the hub: the place where you choose who you will be, wait for your friends, and decide when the night begins.

At its centre stands a table, and on it a small model of the manor under snow. It is the chapter you are about to play, and the way into it.

The manor itself is changing just as quickly. This is the grand staircase of the Grand Hall, seen from the same spot before and after our work.

<div class="post-pair">{% figure "blog/stairs-before.webp", "The grand staircase before the work: bare columns, a dark hall and a checkerboard floor, with only two small lamps lit.", "Before: bare, dark and empty." %}{% figure "blog/stairs-after.webp", "The grand staircase after the work: red marble columns, oak panelling, portraits, candles and a long carpet climbing the steps.", "After: the same place, dressed." %}</div>

Marble columns, oak panelling, portraits, candles and carpets now fill a room that had almost nothing. Not a single step of the staircase moved: only its clothes changed. It is how we work, one piece at a time, looked at in the game itself, again and again.

## What comes next?

We are still choosing what to show you, and we would rather show it when it is ready. The characters in these captures are earlier models, the interface is still in French at this stage, and none of what you see here is final.

In the meantime, come and say hello: our [Discord server](/discord/) is the best place to ask questions, share ideas and tell us what you think.
