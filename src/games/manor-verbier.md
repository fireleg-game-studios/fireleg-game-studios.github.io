You arrive at night before the Verbier manor, frozen on the evening of its grand ball. The park gate closes behind you. The windows are dark, and the great door stands ajar.

Inside, the candles are still burning and the tables are still set. From the cellars to the attic, every floor keeps its own secrets.

Somewhere in the dark, something is laughing.
